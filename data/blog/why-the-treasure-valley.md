Some places are good for one triathlon sport. The Treasure Valley is good for all three, which is part of why we started a club here.

## Swim

Between the Boise River, local reservoirs and the area's pools, there is more than one place to get wet, whatever your comfort level. Pools are a great place to start. Open water takes a little more planning and a lot more care, so go with a group and check conditions before you jump in.

## Bike

You can ride flat and fast on the roads that run along the valley floor, or head up into the foothills when you want a challenge. Group rides are a good way to find the roads that locals use, and the ones they avoid.

## Run

The **Boise River Greenbelt** is the obvious star. It's a paved path that runs along the river for miles, shaded in a lot of places and close to just about everything. Hop on a different stretch every week and you will not get bored. If you prefer dirt, the trails in the foothills are right there.

## The seasons

We get all four. That means a few cold mornings, some hot afternoons and a lot of days that are just right. It also means you will learn to dress in layers, which is a real triathlon skill.

## The people

This is the real reason. A place can have every trail and path imaginable, and training still feels better with other people. Having someone waiting at the park at ten on a Saturday morning makes it a lot easier to get out of bed.

That's why we're here. Come ride, run or swim with us, and tell us your favorite route. We'll add it to the list.
