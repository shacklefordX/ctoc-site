Questions

Do I need to be an experienced triathlete?

No. Beginners are welcome. Come to a session, meet people, and go at your own pace.

Where do sessions start?

Every session on the schedule lists its start location. The next ones start at Merrill Park.

What should I bring?

Water, and the gear for the sport: a helmet and a bike in good working order for rides, and comfortable running shoes for runs.

How do I stay in the loop?

Join the club on Strava and check the schedule page, which is updated whenever new sessions are added.
